title: Stats
order: 20
---
Every character starts from the same base values. Class, race, weapon, armor and potion change them.

- **hp**: health. At 0 the character is out of the fight.
- **attack**: what most damage skills are built on.
- **defense**: skills that mention it take it off the damage they deal. Not every skill does.
- **initiative**: determines acting order in a round
