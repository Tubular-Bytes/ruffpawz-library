---
title: Items and affixes
order: 30
---
A character rolls its class, race, weapon, armor and potion. Every block that can be rolled has the same chance of coming up. Class and race are who the character is, weapon and armor are what it carries, and the potion gives it one more skill.

**Affixes.** Weapons and armor roll affixes: bonuses to a stat, such as attack, defense, initiative or hp. Each affix has tiers, and each tier has a range the value is rolled in, so two items of the same kind can be quite different. An item gets between 1 and 6 affixes, but never more than its kind offers, and the same affix is never rolled twice on one item. Some kinds can only roll the lower or the higher tiers of an affix. The exact rolls and their ranges are shown on the item.

**Skills.** A weapon or an armor offers skills, and you pick up to 3 of them for the character's loadout. Skills are not rolled; the kind of item decides which ones it offers.

**Retired.** Parts of the game that are no longer rolled stay on the characters that already have them.
