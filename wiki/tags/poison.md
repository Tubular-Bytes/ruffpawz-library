Poison skills hurt an enemy right away and then keep hurting them: the poison deals its damage at the start of each of the enemy's turns until it wears off. Armor and damage-taken effects reduce it like any other damage, and poisoning the same enemy twice only renews the poison, it does not stack.

Poison can be cleansed: a cleanse aimed at poison removes it at once. Characters who are immune to poison resist it, and poison already on them pauses for as long as the immunity lasts, while its rounds keep running out.
